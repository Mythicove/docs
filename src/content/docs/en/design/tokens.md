The full token system (primitive colors, semantic roles, typography, spacing, shape, shadow and motion tokens) lives in the [Chinese page](/design/tokens/).

The Chinese page defines three layers (primitive, semantic, component), a blue-violet brand palette with a night-scale dark theme, Inter for body and Space Grotesk for display, a 4px spacing grid, and a signature gradient reserved for the logo, hero and primary CTA.
