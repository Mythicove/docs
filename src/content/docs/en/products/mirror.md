Mythic Mirror is a Yggdrasil-compatible skin station, a modern replacement for Blessing Skin.

- **Yggdrasil auth** (all authlib-injector endpoints) with RSA-signed profiles (1.19+).
- **Skin / cape upload** with PNG validation, slim-model detection, HD skins.
- **Accounts**: registration, email verification, OAuth (GitHub), captcha, invite codes.
- **Premium compatibility**: bind a Microsoft account, keep the real UUID, fall back to Mojang textures.
- **OIDC provider** (Fosite) for SSO across the ecosystem.

See the [product reference](/products/mirror/) and the [plan](/plans/mirror/) (both Chinese), or the [quickstart](/en/getting-started/quickstart/).
