The research conclusions for each product line (why not fork or reuse an existing project) live in the [Chinese page](../../research/why-self-built.md).

In short: Blessing Skin is the only complete open-source skin station and is stuck on PHP 8.1 EOL; Modrinth is the only production-grade open-source mod host and went source-available in 2024; CurseForge is closed source with a restricted API; there is no modern forkable alternative. Meanwhile the useful building blocks (skinview3d, the Yggdrasil spec, crafatar conventions, packwiz) are reusable, and Go fits the Minecraft distribution toolchain.
